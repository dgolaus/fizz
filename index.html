Workflow

From order to delivery.

Step 01 · Open

A ticket opens

A client opens a private ticket. The order starts in its own space, away from public channels.

Step 02 · Queue

The queue updates

The project joins your queue automatically, right next to orders you added from DMs.

Step 03 · Priority

Flexible priority

Optional Rush Priority can move urgent projects ahead of standard orders.

Work moves forward

Update the status as you work and keep private notes hidden from clients.

Features

Built for commission work.

Accept orders

Private tickets in one click

Clients open a private ticket instantly. Each order lives in its own space, separate from public channels.

Manage your workload

Tickets and DMs, one list

Keep ticket orders and DM clients in one queue, with positions, manual entries and optional Rush Priority.

Track project status

From waiting to completed

Move orders from waiting to in progress and completed, keeping your workload clear at every stage.

Keep every record

Transcripts that stay

Messages and attachments remain available in readable transcripts after tickets close.

Also included · private notes · blacklist · add and remove users · slash commands

Tickets

Add a private note
/notes
View the ticket's private notes

Queue

/queue
View the full order queue
/add-queue
Add a DM order to the queue
/remove-queue
Remove a manual entry
/status
Update an order's status and priority
/clear-queue
Clear the whole queue

Moderation

/blacklist
Block a user from opening tickets
/unblacklist
Unblock a user
